Supermodel REDUX Workbench V942 for Mac

1. Extract the ZIP for Apple Silicon or Intel. Requires macOS 15 or later.
2. Double-click Supermodel REDUX Workbench.app in Finder.
3. Select the official Model:Cycles OS 1.13 SysEx from Elektron when prompted.
4. Choose your features, then click Build. Connect the Cycles over USB to flash.

If macOS blocks the first launch, open System Settings > Privacy & Security
and choose Open Anyway for Workbench.

Stop playback and choose CYC for Transfer on the Cycles before flashing.
Press LEVEL/DATA on the Cycles to confirm the install when prompted.

Read Manual.md for the full feature and shortcut guide.
